Rules / explicit assumptions
Explainable by design.
Score and trigger contract
15 signals / 100 points: telemetry 30, support 20, relationships 15, adoption 15, retention 20. Onboarding: 10 / 20 / 15 / 40 / 15.
Thriving ≥85; Healthy ≥70; Watch ≥50; At Risk ≥30; Critical <30. Bands use unrounded scores.
Fresh coverage: High ≥85%, Medium ≥60%, Low ≥40%, Insufficient <40%. Missing values receive a provisional neutral value; they reduce coverage. Inapplicable values redistribute weight only inside their pillar.
P1 >72h and explicit exit intent cap score below 50. Unreconciled exposure ≥1.5× typical flow caps below 70. Healthy / Thriving require ≥60% fresh coverage in every pillar and overall Medium confidence.
Immediate P1 paging is independent of CXI. Recovery is limited to +6 points per week; deterioration is immediate. A better band requires two qualifying runs at least 5 points above its threshold.
Signal weights, thresholds, neutral values and time windows are modeled assumptions requiring calibration. No revenue uplift is inferred.

Role alignment / operating model
One customer journey. Four connected channels.
Independent work sample for the Head of Customer Experience opportunity advertised by Z47. The hiring company is undisclosed; all cases and metrics are synthetic.
AI-led journeys
Onboarding · in-app
Explain the next step, retrieve verification status, detect mismatches and retain specialist ownership. No AI approval of customer eligibility.
Servicing · digital
Authenticate customer scope, retrieve order status, answer with source evidence and record the customer acknowledgment.
Issue resolution · voice and chat
Carry the same case context between channels, reconcile payment or refund status and hand off uncertain tool results without duplicate actions.
Retention · email and in-app
Identify repeat friction, offer an authorized recovery path and respect cancellation requests. Measure return usage against a comparison cohort.
Ownership across business and technology
CX leader: owns journey quality, operating costs, staffing, coaching and recovery accountability.
Product: owns friction removal and self-service journey design.
Engineering: owns identity scope, orchestration, integrations, observability and defect correction.
Operations: owns exception queues, specialist decisions and follow-through.
AI quality: evaluates grounded answers, inappropriate actions, escalation precision, repeat contact and language coverage.
Service agents can explain and retrieve status. Financial changes, identity exceptions and sensitive decisions stay within separately authorized workflows.
